DayCount Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

Data we collect

None. DayCount does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

The D-Days you create (title, date, repeat and calendar settings) are stored only on your iPhone so the app and its Home Screen widget can show them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Tracking, ads and analytics

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
