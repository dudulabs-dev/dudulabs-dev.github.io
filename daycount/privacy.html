DayCount Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

DayCount ("the app") is made by an independent developer, duhyun jo (dudulabs).

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The D-Days you create (title, date, repeat and calendar settings) are stored only on your iPhone, so the app and its Home Screen widget can show them. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Tracking, ads and analytics

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
