Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the dudulabs website (dudulabs-dev.github.io) and lists the policy for each app. Each app has its own policy, which applies to that app.

Apps

  • DayCount — collects no data
  • PhotoPilot — collects no data; camera and photos stay on your device
  • Muscle Memory — collects no data

Our current apps contain no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs. If that ever changes, the app's policy and App Store privacy label will be updated before release.

Website

The website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We delete support emails within 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete information you have sent us by email.
