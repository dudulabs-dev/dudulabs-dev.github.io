Muscle Memory Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

Data we collect

None. Muscle Memory does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your routines, workout sets (exercise, weight, reps, date) and rest settings are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone when your rest timer ends. Nothing is sent to a server.

Health data

The app does not access Apple Health.

Tracking, ads and analytics

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
